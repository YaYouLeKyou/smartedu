Resource Overview

Generative AI video tools are revolutionising how educators and students create multimedia content. From automated explainer videos to deepfake-free character animation, these tools enable anyone to produce professional-quality educational videos without expensive equipment. This resource provides an overview of the best AI video creation tools for educators in 2025, with practical guidance on classroom integration.

"AI video tools democratise content creation, making it possible for every teacher and student to become a filmmaker."

Key Learning Objectives

  • Select appropriate AI video tools for specific educational objectives.
  • Create engaging educational videos using AI-assisted production workflows.
  • Evaluate AI-generated video content for accuracy, bias, and educational value.
  • Teach students media literacy while using AI video tools.
  • Understand copyright, attribution, and ethical use of AI-generated video content.

Suggested Classroom Activities

Activity 1: Explainer Video Project

Students use AI video tools to create 2-minute explainer videos on a curriculum topic. They must include a fact-check section discussing the accuracy of AI-generated content.

Critical Reflection Questions

Discussion prompts:

  • Who owns the copyright of AI-generated video content created by students?
  • How do we verify the accuracy of AI-generated video content before sharing it?
  • What are the risks of students becoming "consumers" of AI-generated media rather than "creators"?
  • How can AI video tools be used to enhance, rather than replace, traditional media literacy education?
  • Should students be required to disclose when they used AI to create video content?
