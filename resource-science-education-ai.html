Transforming Science Education with Digital Innovation

AR/VR, simulations, and AI-driven experiments in science learning

Back to Chapter: Creative
Science Education Intermediate Workshop Module

Resource Overview

This resource was developed during the Smart edu AI Turkey mobility, hosted by the Düzce Milli Egemenlik Bilim ve Sanat Merkezi (BİLSEM). It explores how AI-powered digital tools — including virtual reality cell exploration, iNaturalist for biodiversity observation, and AI-assisted data analysis — can transform science education.

The approach blends science with art ("the Art of Science"), encouraging students to see scientific concepts through a creative lens while developing technical literacy. Students conducted pre- and post-tests to assess learning outcomes and reflect on their AI-assisted learning experience.

"The future scientist must be part artist, part technologist, and part storyteller."

Key Learning Objectives

  • Use AI-powered tools for scientific observation and data collection.
  • Conduct virtual science experiments using AR/VR and simulation tools.
  • Analyse experimental data with AI assistance while maintaining scientific rigour.
  • Integrate artistic creativity into science learning through AI-assisted design.
  • Evaluate the impact of AI tools on science learning outcomes.

AI Tools for Science Education

iNaturalist

AI-powered species identification and biodiversity observation tool. Uses computer vision to identify plants and animals from photos, with a global citizen science community.

VR Cell Exploration

Virtual reality tools that allow students to explore cellular structures in 3D, observing organelles and processes at a microscopic scale.

Khan Academy AI

Adaptive learning platform that uses AI to personalise content delivery, identify knowledge gaps, and recommend targeted practice in science subjects.

AI Data Analysis

Tools that help students collect, visualise, and interpret experimental data, detecting patterns and trends that might be missed by human analysis alone.

Suggested Classroom Activities

Activity 1: Virtual Lab Investigation

Students use VR or simulation tools to conduct a virtual science experiment (e.g., chemical reactions, physics phenomena). Compare results with real-world experiments and discuss limitations of simulation.

Activity 2: Biodiversity Field Study

Using iNaturalist, students observe and identify local flora and fauna. They use AI for identification, then verify results through research and discussion with experts.

Activity 3: Science-Art Fusion Project

Students create AI-assisted visual art, digital designs, or 3D models inspired by scientific concepts. They present how their artistic interpretation enhances understanding of the science.

Critical Reflection Questions

Discussion prompts:

  • How accurate are AI-powered species identification tools, and what are the consequences of errors in scientific research?
  • Can virtual experiments truly replace hands-on laboratory work?
  • How does AI data analysis change the way we teach the scientific method?
  • What ethical considerations arise when AI makes decisions about scientific interpretations?
  • How can we ensure that VR/AR science experiences are accessible to all students?
