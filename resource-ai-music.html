AI in Music: Suno, BandLab, Soundraw and Cultural Fusion

Exploring AI-assisted music composition and cross-cultural creative expression

Back to Chapter: Creative
Creative AI Beginner to Intermediate Workshop Module

Resource Overview

This resource was developed during the Smart edu AI Turkey mobility, where students and teachers from Poland, Türkiye, France, and Spain explored AI-assisted music composition and cultural fusion. Using tools such as Suno, BandLab, and Soundraw, participants created rhythms inspired by scientific themes and blended cultural musical elements through digital production.

The resource demonstrates how AI music tools can support interdisciplinary learning — combining science, culture, and the arts — while fostering creativity, collaboration, and critical thinking about AI's role in creative expression.

"AI in music is not about replacing human creativity; it is about opening new creative possibilities for every learner."

Key Learning Objectives

  • Use AI music tools to compose, arrange, and produce original music.
  • Blend cultural musical traditions using AI-assisted digital production techniques.
  • Integrate music with other subjects (science, history, literature) through AI projects.
  • Critically evaluate AI-generated music for originality, style, and cultural authenticity.
  • Collaborate in international, multicultural teams using shared AI music platforms.

AI Music Tools

Suno

AI-powered music composition platform that generates full songs from text descriptions. Supports multiple languages and musical styles.

BandLab

Collaborative digital audio workstation with AI-assisted mastering, loop generation, and real-time multi-user project sharing.

Soundraw

AI-generated royalty-free music platform with customisable mood, genre, and length parameters for educational and creative projects.

Suggested Classroom Activities

Activity 1: Science-to-Song Project

Students choose a scientific concept (e.g., photosynthesis, the water cycle, Newton's laws) and use AI music tools to create an original song explaining the concept. They present and evaluate how well the music aids understanding.

Activity 2: Cultural Fusion Workshop

Students from different cultural backgrounds collaborate to blend musical traditions using AI tools. They explore how AI interprets and merges different musical styles.

Activity 3: AI Music Critique Circle

Students listen to AI-generated compositions and critically analyse elements like melody, rhythm, emotional expression, and cultural authenticity. They discuss what feels "human" versus "machine-generated."

Critical Reflection Questions

Discussion prompts:

  • Is music generated by AI "real" music, or is something essential lost in the process?
  • How does AI interpret and represent different cultural musical traditions?
  • Can AI music tools help preserve endangered musical traditions, or might they homogenise them?
  • What role should human emotion and experience play in AI-generated music?
  • Who owns the copyright of AI-generated music created by students?
